The Lab Report: your site, scored.

Paste any URL and get a plain-English read on speed, accessibility and search readiness. One score, three bars, the three things worth fixing first.

Public data only. We store nothing except the score, cached for 24 hours.

Why we give this away

Because most businesses have never actually seen their own site measured. They know it feels slow, or that enquiries are thin, but nobody has shown them a number. The Lab Report shows the number in ten seconds, with no form, no email gate and no call booked on the back of it.

If the score is bad and you want it fixed, we do that for a living. If the score is good, you just learned something free. Either outcome is fine by us.

What it measures

  • Performance. How fast the page actually renders on a mid-range phone, not on a developer laptop.
  • Accessibility. Whether the page can be read and used by people with screen readers, low vision or slow connections.
  • Best practices and SEO. The fundamentals that decide whether search engines can read and trust the page.

What it does not do

  • It does not sign you up to anything. There is no email field, because there is nothing to send you.
  • It does not publish your result. Scans are visible only to the person who ran them.
  • It does not replace a real audit. It is a fast, honest first look, not a 40-page PDF.

Powered by

Google PageSpeed Insights, running on free-tier infrastructure. That is why results are cached for 24 hours and rate-limited — if the scanner is busy, try again in a minute.
