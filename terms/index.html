Terms of service.

How we scope, price and hand over work, who owns what, and what happens if something goes wrong. Short, because it should be readable.

Effective Date: November 2026 · The Flow Labs

These terms govern development engagements delivered through www.theflowlabs.online. If anything here directly conflicts with a signed statement of work (SOW) or master services agreement, the signed document takes precedence.

1. Scoping comes before code

Every project begins with a written technical specification: exact features, explicit exclusions, client asset dependencies, and precise completion criteria. We never begin engineering on verbal assumptions. Any post-approval scope modifications are priced and approved in writing beforehand.

2. Fixed pricing, paid in milestone stages

  • Guaranteed Pricing: The agreed quote is the final price for the specified scope. It does not inflate if implementation takes additional founder hours.
  • Milestone Payments: Split into structured checkpoints: an initial deposit, intermediate staging reviews, and final balance upon production handover.
  • Pass-Through Infrastructure: Cloud hosting, domains, third-party LLM inference tokens, and paid fonts are owned directly by the client and passed through at direct cost.

3. Complete intellectual property ownership

Upon settlement of the final milestone invoice, you hold full intellectual property ownership of all custom software, design assets, and database schemas crafted specifically for your build. We deliver access to GitHub repositories and production cloud credentials.

4. Client responsibilities & decision agility

Successful rapid execution requires one designated stakeholder who can approve design prototypes, architectural decisions, and final staging builds in a timely manner.

5. Warranty & post-launch support

Every custom build includes a dedicated post-launch defect warranty window to remediate any bugs that deviate from the agreed technical specification. Post-warranty maintenance is offered via flexible retainer or ad-hoc support agreements.

6. Governing jurisdiction

These terms are governed by the laws of India, under the jurisdiction of competent courts in Bhubaneswar, Odisha.
