INTERVIEW-COPILOT: prepare like the real thing

An open-source AI interview companion that helps candidates prepare, rehearse and improve with real-time coaching and structured feedback.

Status: open source, actively built by our founders.

What it is

Interview prep today is solved by reading blogs and praying. INTERVIEW-COPILOT makes you talk: it runs a live, AI-led interview session, listens while you answer, and coaches you right after, with structure: what worked, what rambled, what to sharpen.

Why we built it

Because our founders sat on both sides of the table. Candidates prepare content, not delivery. One practice partner, available at midnight, that is honest in a way friends will not be, that prep is priceless.

Features

  • Role-based interview sessions (technical, behavioural, domain)
  • Real-time question flow with follow-ups, like a real interviewer
  • Structured post-session feedback: clarity, structure, confidence
  • Improvement tracking across sessions

Stack

  • Python AI pipeline with speech and LLM layers
  • Session and feedback data models
  • Coach UI for interview flow

What it proves about us

AI assistants are more than FAQ bots: they can listen, guide and coach. That same technology backs our AI chatbot development service, just pointed at your business instead of interviews.
