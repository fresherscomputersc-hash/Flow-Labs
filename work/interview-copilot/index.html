01 // The Problem

Static Memorization Fails

Most job seekers prepare by reading static blogs and memorizing answers. In real interviews, they stumble on spontaneous follow-up questions, rambling structure, and lack of real-time stress inoculation.

02 // The Architecture

Adaptive Conversational AI

Engineered with low-latency speech synthesis, dynamic prompt trees, and role-based personas (Staff Engineer, Product Lead, System Architect) that ask challenging follow-ups based on the candidate's actual verbal answers.

03 // The Impact

Objective Rubric Feedback

Provides instant post-session breakdowns: speech clarity, STAR framework adherence, filler word density, and concrete technical depth scores that friends and family cannot objectively give.

Intelligent Capabilities

Simulating Real High-Stakes Pressure

  • Role-Targeted Question Trees: Technical system design, behavioral leadership, and coding architecture scenarios.
  • Real-Time Spoken Interaction: Seamless push-to-talk and voice activation mirroring a genuine Zoom/Meet call.
  • Live Critique Generation: Instant analysis of structural gaps, vague assertions, and conciseness flags.
  • Session Progress Vault: Tracks confidence and response cadence trajectories across repeated practice cycles.
