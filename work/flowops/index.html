01 // The Problem

Queue Congestion & Burnout

Support teams lose 60% of their bandwidth answering repetitive questions. High-severity tier-1 escalations languish in static chronological queues, and leadership lacks real-time insight into spiking customer frustration points.

02 // The Architecture

Deterministic Triage Engine

Every incoming payload passes through an NLP classifier for sentiment, urgency, and category. Routine queries get an instant contextual response draft queued for 1-click human approval, while critical anomalies alert on-call staff instantly.

03 // The Impact

65% Faster Ticket Resolution

Human agents focus exclusively on complex edge cases. Response times drop from hours to minutes, repetitive inquiries are resolved in seconds, and an automated weekly trend engine flags recurring product bugs before they spread.

Pipeline Protocol

The FlowOps Processing Loop

  • 01 · Ingestion & Sanitization: Webhook intake from Zendesk, Intercom, or custom mailboxes with automated PII masking.
  • 02 · Intent & Urgency Matrix: Deep classification assigns urgency scores and identifies churn-risk language.
  • 03 · Contextual Draft Synthesis: Grounded retrieval from company documentation synthesizes an exact, brand-consistent reply.
  • 04 · Human-in-the-Loop Approval: Operators approve, edit, or reject drafts in a single keystroke before transmission.
