QR Business Platform: one scan, one page, every business

A centrally operated multi-business QR platform built by our founders. Each participating business gets a stable QR code and a fast, mobile-friendly digital page that customers reach by scanning. No business-owner dashboard, no customer accounts: our team runs it end to end.

Status: live product. A passcode-protected demo runs right on this site at theflowlabs.online/qr - ask us for the demo passcode and explore the real console. Demo data is synthetic and resets on boot.

What it is

Many small businesses have no usable web presence, or a slow one buried three clicks deep. The QR Business Platform gives each business one stable printed QR code that resolves to a lightning-fast mobile page: menu, services, photos, timings, contact and offers. When the content changes, the QR code does not, so printed materials never go stale.

The problem

  • Small businesses print menus and pamphlets that are outdated the week after printing.
  • Building a real website is too expensive or too slow for many local businesses, so they stay unreachable online.
  • Owners do not want another dashboard to maintain; they want someone to just keep it current.

How it works

Our internal team creates and maintains each business page in a central console: businesses, locations, content sections, media, leads and publish states. Customers scan the stable QR code, the platform resolves it to the right business page, and every scan, view, click and contact tap is tracked so the business learns what customers actually look at. Publishing is versioned with approval, rollback and an audit trail.

Stack

  • Next.js 15 (App Router) with TypeScript
  • SQLite via better-sqlite3, Postgres-portable schema, zod validation everywhere
  • Server-side QR artwork generation (SVG/PNG) so codes are stable and printable
  • Role-based staff auth, scan/view/click analytics, versioned publishing with audit
  • Deployed on Vercel, served under this website at /qr

What it proves about us

We ship complete products, not prototypes: multi-role auth, versioned content, analytics, an audit log and a verified end-to-end suite. The same engineering goes into a custom platform we build for you.
