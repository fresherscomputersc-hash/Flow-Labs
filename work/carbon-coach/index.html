Carbon Coach: habits, tracked honestly

An open-source TypeScript app that tracks personal carbon footprints, shows live metrics and nudges weekly progress in a guilt-free tone.

Status: open source, built by our founders.

What it is

Most carbon apps guilt-trip you into closing them. Carbon Coach does the opposite: it tracks your footprint in a clean dashboard, shows genuinely alive metrics, and nudges you again next week with improvements, not lectures.

Features

  • Daily footprint tracking across key habits
  • Live metrics dashboard, no spreadsheet vibes
  • Weekly progress nudges, framed as encouragement
  • Progress history, so improvement becomes visible

Why we built it

Two reasons. First, sustainability tools in India feel imported and detached from local daily life. Second, it is a compact product that lets us practice the entire loop: fast front end, real data, and clean feedback UX, without enterprise paperwork.

Stack

  • TypeScript app, component-driven UI
  • Metric engine with weekly aggregation
  • Analytics and progress visualisations

What it proves about us

Front-end craft and live data handling. The same coding standards go into every website and web app we build for a business.
