Droovee: book a chauffeur, drive your own car

A live chauffeur-booking platform in Bhubaneswar built by our founders. On-demand professional drivers: verified profiles, transparent pricing, and instant trip requests.

Status: live product at droovee.vercel.app. Built in-house.

What it is

Droovee lets people book a professional chauffeur to drive them in their own car. Standalone drivers for a night out, a long trip, or when the boss in you wants to sleep in the back seat. It was born from a very simple question: why has nobody made this easy yet?

The problem

  • Getting a professional driver for your OWN car is a phone-book process in most Indian cities.
  • Bookings happen over calls, papers and trust-your-uncle networks. Zero reliability, zero records.
  • Drivers are a safety decision, so users want verified profiles and visible pricing.

How we built it

Droovee is a full web application: driver profiles with verification states, trip requests with instant matching, transparent fare display before confirm, and a booking flow that a first-time user completes without hand-holding. Payments and communication rails are wired into the flow.

Stack

  • Web app: React-based front end, Node/JS services
  • Booking engine with driver matching states
  • Payments and pricing display
  • Deployed on Vercel (droovee.vercel.app)
