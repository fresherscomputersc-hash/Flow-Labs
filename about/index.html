Founder-led · In-house

Founder-led engineering. Built in-house.

The Flow Labs is a three-person engineering studio. We design, build and ship interfaces, systems and applied intelligence — and you work with the people writing the code.

The Flow Labs was founded on a straightforward principle: businesses deserve direct access to the engineers building their software. Instead of working through sales representatives and outsourced development teams, our clients collaborate directly with the founders who design and write the code. Droovee, our live chauffeur booking platform, is a product of this hands-on engineering approach.

Who you actually work with

  • Smruti Ranjan — Founder. Architects the systems and AI infrastructure behind every project, and leads scoping on every build.
  • Biswash — Co-Founder. Builds the platforms, APIs and backend infrastructure that have to hold up under real load.
  • Arpita — Co-Founder. Shapes the interfaces, user experience and visual identity across every product we ship.

Three people, no account layer. Every project is handled by the same three, which is exactly why we cap the month at four builds.

How we work

  • You talk to the people building it. No account managers, no handoffs, no “our developer will get back to you”.
  • Fixed price before code. Scope, timeline and price agreed first. No surprise invoices mid-project.
  • Weekly demos. You watch the product grow in real, clickable versions, not slide decks.
  • We ship in-house. Interfaces, systems and AI are built by us. If a task does not fit our skills, we say so instead of subcontracting quietly.

Where we are

We work remotely with teams across India and abroad, and meet in person when a project genuinely calls for it. Our studio address is on the contact page.

What we will NOT do

  • Promise rankings and overnight SEO miracles. We set honest expectations instead.
  • Take a project we are not the right team for. You will get a straight answer in the first call, possibly with a referral.
  • Inflate our history. What you see in our lab is our actual work, labelled honestly by status (live, in-house, open source).

Our approach

We believe in transparency, rapid execution and technical craft. We agree on fixed pricing before code starts, demonstrate progress through weekly interactive builds, and deliver maintainable software that solves real operational problems.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who are the founders?

Smruti Ranjan (Founder), Biswash (Co-Founder) and Arpita (Co-Founder). Three people, no account layer — you work with the same three from first call to handover.

Is The Flow Labs an agency?

We are a studio, not an agency: the founders do the work personally, with fixed-price projects and no outsourced middle layer.

How many projects do you take on?

Four a month, and no more. That is the honest ceiling for a team that does not subcontract. Current availability is on the contact page.

Speak directly with the engineering team

The most effective way to start is a scoping conversation. Bring your requirements, and we will provide an honest assessment of scope, timeline, and pricing.
