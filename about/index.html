Founder-led · Bhubaneswar

Two founders. One stack. Zero outsourcing.

The Flow Labs is a small, founder-led engineering studio in Bhubaneswar. This page is the honest version of who you are hiring.

The Flow Labs started the way most real products do: somebody said “bhai ek idea hai” at 2AM, and instead of quoting an agency, the founders built it themselves. Droovee, our live chauffeur booking platform, is a direct result.

How we work

  • You talk to the people building it. No account managers, no handoffs, no “our developer will get back to you”.
  • Fixed price before code. Scope, timeline and price agreed first. No surprise invoices mid-project.
  • Weekly demos. You watch the product grow in real, clickable versions, not slide decks.
  • We ship in-house. AI, web, automation and software are built by us. If a task does not fit our skills, we say so instead of subcontracting quietly.

Where we are

Based in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, India. Local in presence, serious in ambition: we take projects across India, remotely, and meet in person around Bhubaneswar when the project calls for it.

What we will NOT do

  • Promise rankings and overnight SEO miracles. We set honest expectations instead.
  • Take a project we are not the right team for. You will get a straight answer in the first call, possibly with a referral.
  • Inflate our history. What you see in our lab is our actual work, labelled honestly by status (live, in-house, open source).

Why the meme energy

Because engineering is serious and agencies are boring. You heard us joke about Excel’s funeral; watch our weekly demos and you will see why the work is anything but a joke.
