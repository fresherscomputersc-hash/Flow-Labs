Privacy policy

What we collect when you enquire, why, how long we keep it and how to make us forget you.

Last updated: 2026-11-05. This applies to www.theflowlabs.online and every enquiry or Lab Report you send us from it.

What we collect

  • What you type into our forms: your name, email or phone, project type, project description and budget range.
  • Technical metadata, automatically: your browser’s user-agent string and the page source tag (for understanding where enquirers came from).
  • Anonymous page views: we count visits to understand which pages are useful. This is cookie-less: no cookies, no localStorage, no fingerprinting, no IP address stored, and no personal data attached.

We do not run advertising trackers, third-party analytics, or analytics cookies.

The Lab Report

The Lab Report is a free public-URL scanner. It fetches the page you paste and scores it with Google PageSpeed Insights, then shows you the result. We store the URL you scanned and the returned scores for 24 hours so repeat scans are fast and so we do not hammer the free API. We do not store your IP address beyond the five-minute rate limit check, and we never attach a scan to an enquiry unless you send one. Nothing about your site is published.

Analytics in plain language

Each page view creates one anonymous row: the path, the referring site’s domain, a coarse device category (mobile, tablet, desktop) and a timestamp. We cannot identify you from it, and it is never linked to an enquiry you submit. If your browser sends Do Not Track, we skip the count entirely.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to your enquiry with scope, timeline and pricing.
  • To understand which of our services attract interest, in aggregate.

Where it lives and who sees it

Submissions are stored in a Supabase database controlled by us, and a copy of each new enquiry reaches our own inbox by email. It is read by The Flow Labs founders only. We do not sell your data, share it with marketers, or subscribe you to anything.

How long we keep it

Active enquiries are kept while the project conversation is alive. If you tell us (or a reasonable period passes without contact), we delete the enquiry record.

Your choices

  • Ask us anything about your data: hello@theflowlabs.online.
  • Ask us to delete your enquiry and we will do it, no interrogation.
  • Send enquiries without the form: just email or DM us directly.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top changes. That is all the ceremony you get, because that is all the ceremony exists.
