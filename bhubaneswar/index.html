The Flow Labs is a founder-led engineering studio based in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. We design the interface, engineer the system behind it, and wire in the AI that removes the repetitive work. Every project is quoted individually with a fixed price before code starts, and the source code is handed over at the end.

What we build for businesses in Bhubaneswar

  • Websites and web apps. Fast, phone-first sites and product interfaces that load quickly and convert. See web development.
  • Custom software. Internal tools, operations dashboards, APIs and data pipelines built around how your team actually works. See custom software development.
  • AI automation. Ticket triage, document processing, lead routing and reporting that runs on its own schedule. See AI automation.
  • AI assistants. A chat assistant trained on your business that answers customers and captures enquiries at any hour. See AI chatbot development.

Why work with a studio here

  • You can meet the people doing the work. For projects in and around Bhubaneswar, we can start with a face-to-face scoping session instead of a video call.
  • Same timezone, direct access. No account managers and no handoffs. You talk to the founders building your product, on IST, when decisions need to be made.
  • The work stays yours. A fixed quote before we start, weekly demos on a real build, and the repository in your hands at handover.

Working with us across India

Most of our clients are outside Odisha. The process is the same whether you are in Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi or Bhubaneswar: a written scope, a fixed price before code, a clickable build every week, and a handover that leaves you independent of us. The services overview explains what each engagement includes.

Working with us internationally

We also run projects with teams outside India, entirely remotely. Everything is written down, demos are recorded, and we keep a shared build you can open at any hour. If your team is in another timezone, we agree a short overlap window for decisions and keep the rest async.

Who we usually work with

Founders and small teams with one specific operational problem: a booking flow that leaks enquiries, a back office held together by spreadsheets, a support inbox nobody can keep up with, or a product idea that needs a real first version. We are a three-person studio and we take on four builds a month, so the work stays close to the people who scoped it.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you only work with clients in Bhubaneswar?

No. Bhubaneswar is where the studio is based. Most projects run remotely with clients across India and abroad, and local projects can include in-person sessions when they help.

Can we meet in person before starting?

If you are in or around Bhubaneswar, yes. We can meet to understand the problem and scope the work before anything is quoted.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We work remotely with teams outside India, keep decisions in writing and share a clickable build every week, so time zones rarely slow a project.

How is pricing decided?

Every project is quoted individually. You get a written scope and a fixed price before code starts, so there are no hourly surprises.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. The quote includes milestones and a delivery date, and you see progress on a real build every week.

Have a project in Bhubaneswar or elsewhere?

Tell us what you are building. We reply within 48 hours with an honest view of scope, timeline and price.
