# ThreeUI Master Redesign Plan: The Flow Labs
**A Complete Architectural & Aesthetic Overhaul Across the Entire Website**

## 1. Vision & ThreeUI Design Philosophy

The Flow Labs will be completely transformed from a traditional static web presence into an **interactive, spatial, high-end digital engineering studio** inspired by the design language of **ThreeUI (`threeui.com`)**, **Design+Code**, and modern creative engineering studios.

### Core ThreeUI Signatures Being Implemented
1. **Interactive WebGL & GLSL Shader Canvases:**
   - Procedural 3D fluid wave fields, particle matrix grids, and chromatic dispersion lighting reacting dynamically to pointer velocity and scroll.
2. **Spatial Glass & Acrylic Bento Architecture:**
   - Asymmetrical, high-density modular Bento Grids featuring translucent frosted acrylic cards, refractive specular highlight borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), and multi-layer depth.
3. **Tactile Physics & Micro-Interactions:**
   - **3D Card Perspective Tilt:** Interactive cards smoothly tilt in 3D perspective (`perspective(1000px) rotateX(...) rotateY(...)`) following cursor coordinates.
   - **Magnetic Buttons & Badges:** Interactive buttons elastic-snap to the cursor with spring physics.
   - **Perimeter Light Beams:** Glowing gradient outlines that follow the exact angle of the user's cursor around cards.
4. **Precision Telemetry & Live HUD:**
   - Monospace tabular numerals, pulsing radar status dots, real-time build slot meters, live IST studio clock, and latency/uptime telemetry.
5. **Radical Structural Freedom:**
   - Zero obligation to keep outdated HTML containers or plain bulleted text dumps. Every single page is rebuilt from scratch with modern spatial layouts and zero clutter.
