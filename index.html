06
FAQ

Straight answers.

How much does a project cost?

Every project is priced individually and quoted as a fixed, all-in number after discovery — before any code is written. There is no rate card, because no two builds are the same. If the scope changes, we agree the new price in writing first.

How many projects do you take at once?

Four per month, and no more. It is the honest ceiling for a three-person team that does not subcontract. When the month is full we start a waiting list rather than quietly spread thin.

How long does delivery take?

Single automations and landing pages ship in days. Interfaces run two to four weeks. Platforms run four to ten weeks and ship in phases, so something working is in your hands early.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. We work with businesses across India and abroad over weekly video demos and short written updates. Time zone has not been a blocker yet.

Who owns the code?

You do. Every project ends with the repository, deployment access and documentation handed over. No lock-in, no licence fee to keep using your own product.

How is data handled?

We deploy onto your cloud accounts and databases wherever possible, enforce role-based access, and retain no proprietary business data after handover.
