FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Straightforward answers regarding our engineering model, timelines, and commercial structure.

How much does a custom software or automation project cost?

Pricing is determined by the scope of work, technical complexity, and integrations required. We provide a transparent, fixed-price quote following our initial discovery discussion, before any code is written. There are no unexpected invoices or hidden fees.

How long does it take to deliver and launch?

Targeted automations and focused landing pages typically ship within days. Larger custom applications and multi-system integrations are deployed in phases so your team can test and derive value from working software early.

Will AI automation replace our existing team members?

No. Our automation solutions eliminate repetitive manual overhead—such as copy-pasting data, repetitive ticket replies, and routine inquiries—freeing your staff to focus on high-touch client relationships and strategic operations.

Do you work with organizations outside Bhubaneswar?

Yes. Although The Flow Labs is headquartered in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, we collaborate remotely with businesses, startups, and institutions across India, using structured weekly video demos and clear asynchronous progress tracking.

How do you ensure data security and privacy?

We deploy directly onto your existing cloud accounts and databases wherever possible. We enforce strict role-based access controls, and retain no customer or proprietary business data on our systems after handover.

What ongoing support is provided after go-live?

Every deployment includes complete source code access, architectural documentation, and a dedicated post-launch support window for maintenance, monitoring, and minor refinements.
