AI automation for businesses in Bhubaneswar

We connect AI to repetitive operational processes so your team can focus on core business growth. Scoped, built, and shipped in-house with fixed pricing.

AI automation is not a robot in a suit. It is chat assistants, document processing, ticket triage, data handover and reporting that quietly do work your team currently does by hand. We build these as working systems: connected to your tools, running on schedule, logging what they did.

What this looks like for a business

  • Customer support automation. Every incoming question is classified, answered when it is routine, and escalated to a human when it matters. Our own FlowOps console does exactly this for support teams.
  • Document and data processing. Quotations, forms, invoices and enquiries arrive as chaos. We build pipelines that read them, extract what matters and file it where your team works.
  • Workflow automation. Handovers, follow-up reminders, lead routing and status reporting, compiled into one reliable pipeline instead of a dozen sticky notes.
  • AI integrations and assistants. A chat assistant trained on your website and business documentation, handling customer questions 24/7, wired into your site or internal tools.

Problems we hear all the time

  • The same ten questions eat your front desk hours every single day.
  • Leads arrive on WhatsApp, Instagram and email, and no single person owns them.
  • Someone retypes data between two tools that should simply talk to each other.
  • Nobody can see today’s numbers without asking two departments.

If any of that sounds familiar, that is exactly the territory this service covers.

Who builds it

No “our implementation team” appears later. The founders who scope your automation write it, demo it weekly and hand it over with documentation. See the proof in our lab: Drishti for operations intelligence and the BHOOMI stack for document and data workflows.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does AI automation cost?

It depends on how many workflows you automate and which tools need connecting. A focused single-workflow build costs less than most websites; multi-system builds are scoped in phases. Either way the price is fixed before code starts.

Will AI automation replace my staff?

No. It replaces the boring 60% of people’s day. Your front desk still handles the sensitive conversations; the automation handles the repeated ones.

What if my data is sensitive?

We build on your accounts wherever possible, restrict what the AI can access, and keep no customer data in our own systems after handover.

Do you work with businesses outside Bhubaneswar?

Yes. Bhubaneswar is home, but we build for teams across Odisha and all of India, remote with weekly video demos.

How fast can something go live?

A single-workflow automation typically ships in days, not months. Bigger builds are phased so you get value early.

Ready to automate repetitive operations?

Tell us which workflows consume your team's time. We will scope an automated solution with clear milestones and fixed pricing.
