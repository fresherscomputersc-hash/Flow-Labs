Web development for businesses in Bhubaneswar

Websites and web applications that load quickly, look polished, and convert visitors into customers. Built and maintained in-house.

Your website is the one employee that talks to every customer before you do. It should load fast, say clearly what you do, and make the next step obvious. That is what we build: clean, responsive sites and web apps for businesses, clinics, gyms, schools and startups.

What we build

  • Business websites. A fast, credible, mobile-first site for your business with real booking or enquiry paths.
  • Startup and landing pages. One page, one job: explain the product and convert the visitor. We ship these in days.
  • Custom web applications. Booking flows, client portals, admin panels. See Droovee, our live chauffeur booking platform.
  • Performance and maintenance. Speed fixes, bug fixes, content updates and post-launch support.

Why business websites here usually underperform

  • They are built once and never touched again, on hosting that breaks.
  • They load on mobile like the designer never saw a phone.
  • They never say the one thing customers actually want to know: what it costs, how to book, when you are open.

We fix exactly those three things: real maintenance, phone-first design, and copy that answers what the customer is actually asking.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost in Bhubaneswar?

Simple business sites and landing pages sit at the lower end of a rough ₹25k to ₹1L range. Custom web apps cost more because they involve real product engineering. You get an exact fixed quote after the first scoping call.

How long does it take to build a website?

A focused business site or landing page typically ships in days. Larger web apps run in phases: first something you can point at, then the full build.

Will my website show up on Google?

Being indexable and fast is the entry ticket, and every site we ship passes it (metadata, performance, mobile-first). Ranking takes longer and depends on your market. We set honest expectations instead of fake guarantees.

Do you maintain the site after launch?

Yes. Handover includes documentation and a support window; ongoing maintenance is available and priced transparently.

Can you connect my enquiry form to WhatsApp/email/CRM?

Yes. Form submissions can reach you by email alert, stored in a database you control, or routed onward. You pick the setup in scoping.
