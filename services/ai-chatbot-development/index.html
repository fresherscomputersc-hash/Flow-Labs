What we build

  • Website FAQ assistants. Trained on your site, docs, price lists and policies. Answers repeat questions around the clock.
  • Booking and enquiry assistants. Collect the lead without ever asking the customer to fill “another boring form”: name, service, time window, contact details.
  • Internal assistants. For your own team: find SOPs, check process steps, answer “how do we handle X” in one tap.
  • Multi-channel. Your website, plus wherever it makes sense of your customer journeys (Instagram, WhatsApp, web widget).

Honest limits

An AI assistant is only as good as its source content. If your information is wrong or stale, it will faithfully repeat nonsense. That is why every build includes a content-grounding step: we assemble the sources first, then wire the assistant. And sensitive cases always route to a human.

Frequently asked

What is an AI chatbot and how is it different from a normal one?

A normal chatbot shows predefined options and canned answers. An AI chatbot reads your own documents and past conversations, understands free-form questions, and answers in natural language, escalating to a human when the situation needs one.

How long does it take to build?

A basic FAQ assistant can be live in days once your content is gathered. Multi-channel or booking assistants take longer, scoped in phases.

Will it hallucinate answers?

Guarded against by design: it is grounded in your sources, says when it does not know, and sensitive questions are routed to a human instead of guessed at.

Does it work in English and Odia/Hindi?

Yes, assistants handle multilingual questions. What matters is the quality of your source content, which we help you assemble.

How much does an AI chatbot cost?

Depends on channels and the depth of your content. A focused assistant is a small project, priced fixed upfront like everything we do.
