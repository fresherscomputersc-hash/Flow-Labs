Custom software development in Bhubaneswar

Internal tools, operational dashboards, and management systems tailored to how your business actually runs.

Every business runs its real operations somewhere: a spreadsheet, three WhatsApp groups and one person’s memory. It works until it does not. We build the software that replaces that fragility: internal tools, dashboards, booking and management systems built around your process, not around some vendor’s feature checklist.

What we build

  • Internal business tools. The app your team opens every morning: intake, tracking, job cards, attendance, whatever your process actually is.
  • Management dashboards. One screen that shows today’s revenue, pipeline or plant status without assembling it manually. See Drishti, our operations intelligence console.
  • Property and verification workflows. See the BHOOMI stack: maps, verification and property data systems.
  • APIs and-backend services. The connecting tissue between tools you already pay for.

Custom vs ready-made, in one honest line

Buy ready-made when your process is standard. Go custom when your process is your advantage, or when you are paying five tools to half-do one job. We will tell you when NOT to build custom, and that honesty is part of the service.

Stack and capabilities

React/Node or Python backends, Postgres or Supabase for data, Vercel or cloud servers for deployment, and role-based access so the right people see the right things. We design the database first: data models outlive interfaces.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does custom software cost?

It scales with what you are actually building, from focused internal tools to multi-phase products. Small tools sit in the same ballpark as a serious website. You get a fixed price per phase before code starts.

How long does custom software take?

Focused tools ship in weeks. Larger systems are phased: first something you can click in weeks, then depth in later phases.

Can you work with our existing systems?

Yes. Integrating with what you already run (Excel, Tally, CRMs, custom APIs) is usually the correct first move, not a rebuild from scratch.

What happens when we want changes after launch?

Post-launch changes are normal. Handover includes documentation and a support window; changes after that are priced transparently, not by surprise.

Do we need our own servers?

Usually no. We deploy on managed cloud (Vercel, Supabase, or a cloud server you own) and hand you full ownership of the code and data.
