The Structural Challenge

Opaque Land Due Diligence

Property verification in India has traditionally relied on fragmented municipal portals, unindexed deed scans, and hearsay. Key records are siloed across disconnected government databases, making verification slow and prone to catastrophic legal surprises.

BHOOMI unifies these fragmented sources into a single verifiable API layer, providing instant parcel lineage and ownership confidence scores.
