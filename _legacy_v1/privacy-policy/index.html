Privacy policy.

What we collect when you enquire, why, how long we keep it and how to make us forget you.

Effective Date: November 2026 · The Flow Labs

This privacy policy applies to www.theflowlabs.online and every project enquiry or Lab Report you submit through our platform.

What we collect

  • Information you provide directly: Your name, email or phone number, project category, project requirements description, and estimated budget range.
  • Automated technical metadata: Coarse user-agent string and referring URL parameters to understand which channels introduce new prospective clients.
  • Anonymous page views: We record aggregate visitor counts. This system is completely cookieless: zero cookies, zero localStorage tracking, zero device fingerprinting, and zero personal identifiers.

We do not operate third-party ad networks, tracking pixels, or surveillance scripts.

The Lab Report Diagnostics

The Lab Report is a free diagnostic scanner. It fetches public URLs and measures them against Google PageSpeed Insights APIs. We store the scanned URL and benchmark score for 24 hours in a temporary cache to prevent API rate-limiting. We never store personal IP addresses beyond short rate-limit checks, and scan outcomes are never attached to client records unless you explicitly submit an inquiry.

Why we collect this information

  • To review your requirements and reply within 48 hours with scope, timeline, and pricing proposals.
  • To analyze in aggregate which service offerings generate market demand.

Data storage and security

Submissions are transmitted securely over TLS and persisted in a Supabase PostgreSQL instance protected by Row Level Security. Notifications route directly to the founders (Smruti, Biswash, and Arpita). We will never sell, rent, or distribute your information to third-party marketers.

Retention and deletion

Active project leads are retained during scoping discussions. If a project does not move forward or reasonable time passes without response, records are purged. You can request immediate erasure at any point by emailing hello@theflowlabs.online.
